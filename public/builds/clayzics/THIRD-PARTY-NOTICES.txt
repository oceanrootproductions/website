## History

Until 2026-10-14 this folder held `js/mikktspace.module.js`, the WASM build of a Rust port distributed with three.js (MIT), with
two local patches to its glue. It lived under wasm32's address ceiling and never gave its heap back, so the importer skipped every
part over 65,535 vertices — 61 of the featurelab island's 118 source parts baked tangent-less, and after objects were merged
(docs/submeshes-plan.md S6) a constant placeholder stood in for them. The reference C code replaced it (docs/tangents-plan.md);
its tangents and the weld after them were measured byte-identical to the old module's on every model of sample/ and featurelab/
(tools/bakeharness.ts: 237/237 bakes, 880 files).
